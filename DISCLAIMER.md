## We do not redistribute the game

## Use at your own risk

- The projects are provided **"as is", without any warranty**. Mods change how the game runs. Things can break.
- **Back up first.** Before installing or updating TESL: Everlasting, or enabling mods, copy your game folder's
  `.offline-user-data` folder (your offline profile and settings) somewhere safe. Keep backups of your own mods too.
- Only install mods from people you trust. TESL Forge refuses packages with program code or paths outside the mod, but
  no checker is perfect.

## Fair play: no cheating against people. Ever.

- Cheats (the F10 cheat options), gameplay mods (new cards, rules, decks, modes, stories) and AI agent or LLM players
  are for **offline play against the in-game AI only**.
- Online, TESL: Everlasting loads **only local visual and audio mods** (remastered art, sounds, music, boards that only
  you see) and graphics options. No cheats and no gameplay changes ever load in an online launch.
- **Anti-cheat:** if a client with the offline engine or cheat code tries to connect to a remote match (another player's
  game or a match server), TESL: Everlasting's *OfflineFairPlay* check **force-closes the game**. This is deliberate and
  cannot be switched off in the mod.
- This applies to humans and to AI agents/LLMs alike: no cheating against other players by anyone, ever. Please respect
  other communities' servers and rules.

## TESL-Reborn

[TESL-Reborn](https://tesl-reborn.com) is a separate community project that brings back human-vs-human online play. We
think it is genuinely wonderful for fans who want to play against other people, and our mod coexists with it. We are not
affiliated with TESL-Reborn: it has its own development, support and rules. **Please never report problems with TESL:
Everlasting or TESL Forge to the TESL-Reborn team.** Use this project's issue tracker instead.
