# Getting started: your first mod

This walks you from install to seeing your own card in the game.

## 1. Set up

1. Install *The Elder Scrolls: Legends* and **TESL: Everlasting** (see its Getting started guide). Start the game offline
   once so your profile exists.
2. Run `TESL-Forge-Setup-1.0.0.exe` (no administrator prompt, no Python needed), then open **TESL Forge** from the
   Start menu. If it can't find the game in your Steam libraries, it asks for the game folder once. Your mods live in
   `Documents\TESL Forge`.
   *Or, with the zip:* install [Python 3.10 or newer](https://www.python.org/downloads/), unzip `TESL-Forge-1.0.0.zip`
   outside the game folder, double-click **Set up TESL Forge.cmd** once, then **Open TESL Forge.cmd**.
3. TESL Forge opens in its own window and records its location so TESL: Everlasting can find your mods. A short tour shows you around (press `?` any time for help).
4. Optional: install the sample mod. On **Load order & compatibility**, drop `TESL-Forge-Sample-Mod-1.0.0.zip` on
   *Install a mod*.
5. Optional: pick a theme and text size at the bottom of the sidebar.

## 2. Create a mod

Click **＋** next to *Mods*. Choose a starter: an empty mod, a copy of another mod, or a mod with a first card, story,
mode or AI opponent ready to edit. Give it a name ("My First Mod"); the folder id fills itself in. Add your name as author and
a short description. **Create**. The mod's **Overview** opens: counts, checks and history.

## 3. Make a card

1. Open **Cards**.
2. Name: *Ember Wyrmling*. Template: search *Fiery Imp* and pick it. The template gives the card its type, frame, sounds
   and, unless you change them, its rules.
3. Cost 2, Power 2, Health 2. Rarity: Rare.
4. Rules: keep the template's, take another card's rules (*rules from*), or add keywords (Guard, Charge, Ward, ...) and
   abilities from other cards with your own numbers.
5. Drop a square picture (1024×1024 or larger, subject centred with headroom). The previews show how the hand and the
   board will crop it.
6. **Save**. The card appears in the list at the top.

## 4. Make a deck

1. Open **Decks**. Name it.
2. Search cards on the right and click to add copies. The checks show the game's rules: 50–70 cards with one or two
   attributes (75–100 with three), at most 3 copies, 1 for unique cards.
3. Pick a banner card. **Save deck**.

## 5. Check and switch on

1. **Overview**: the *Check* badge should say "ready to play". If not, it lists what to fix.
2. Switch **Active in TESL: Everlasting** on.
3. Start the game offline (or press **Play test**, which starts it with your mod and then shows what loaded). Your
   card is in **Collection** and your deck in your deck list, owned as a playset.
4. **Make game previews** (on Cards, Decks, Card backs or Boards) shows your content drawn by the game itself (close the game first).

## 6. Change, undo, share

- Change any item later with its **Edit** button; **History** on the Overview undoes a step.
- Export the mod as a package on **Load order & compatibility** and share the `.zip`.
